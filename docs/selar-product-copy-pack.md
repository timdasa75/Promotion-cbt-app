## Short Product Description

Monthly access to full Promotion CBT practice for Federal Civil Service promotion exam preparation, including all core topics, mock exams, analytics, revision tools, and cloud sync.

## Full Product Description

Promotion CBT Premium is built for candidates preparing seriously for Nigerian Federal Civil Service promotion exams.

With a monthly subscription, you get access to the full CBT practice experience: all core topics, structured mock exams, progress analytics, focused revision tools, and cloud sync across your sessions. The app helps you practise consistently, identify weak areas, revisit missed questions, and build confidence before exam day.

This is ideal if you want a practical study companion that keeps your preparation organised and measurable instead of relying only on scattered past questions or last-minute reading.

After payment, return to the Promotion CBT app and submit your Selar order reference from your profile page. Premium access is activated automatically once your payment is confirmed; otherwise it is reviewed and activated by an admin.

## FAQ Copy

### Is this an official government product?

No. Promotion CBT is an independent study and practice tool. It is not an official government platform and does not represent or claim endorsement by any government agency.

### What happens after I pay?

After payment on Selar, return to the Promotion CBT app and submit your Selar order reference from your profile page. Premium activates automatically when automatic verification is enabled.

### How long does activation take?

Use the same email on Selar and in the app to make automatic verification instant.

### What do I get with Premium?

Premium gives access to all available core topics, mock exams, progress analytics, review tools, and cloud sync for supported accounts.

### Can I cancel the subscription?

Yes. Selar manages the subscription billing. Use your Selar account or payment email to manage cancellation according to Selar's subscription settings.

### What if I used a different email on Selar?

Submit your Selar order reference in the app and use the same email on Selar and in the app. If the emails do not match, automatic verification cannot confirm the order and it will be queued for manual review.
