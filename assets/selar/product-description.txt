Promotion CBT Premium is built for candidates preparing seriously for Nigerian Federal Civil Service promotion exams.

With a monthly subscription, you get access to the full CBT practice experience: all core topics, structured mock exams, progress analytics, focused revision tools, and cloud sync across your sessions. The app helps you practise consistently, identify weak areas, revisit missed questions, and build confidence before exam day.

This is ideal if you want a practical study companion that keeps your preparation organised and measurable instead of relying only on scattered past questions or last-minute reading.

What you get:

- Access to all available Promotion CBT core topics
- Premium mock exam practice
- Topic-based practice sessions
- Performance analytics and progress tracking
- Review tools for missed questions
- Focused revision support
- Cloud sync for signed-in users
- Ongoing access while the monthly subscription remains active

After payment, return to the Promotion CBT app and submit your Selar order reference from your profile page. Your premium access will be reviewed and activated by an admin.

Important: Use the same email address on Selar that you use to sign in to the Promotion CBT app. This helps us match your payment to your account and activate premium access faster.
